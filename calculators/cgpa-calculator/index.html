What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is commonly used by educational institutions to represent a student's overall academic performance.

How is CGPA calculated?

When subjects have different credit values, CGPA can be calculated using a credit-weighted average of the grade points.

CGPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit) ÷ Σ(Credits)

Example

Suppose a student has three subjects:

Mathematics: Grade Point 9, Credits 4
Physics: Grade Point 8, Credits 3
English: Grade Point 10, Credits 2

The weighted calculation is:

CGPA = (9 × 4 + 8 × 3 + 10 × 2) ÷ (4 + 3 + 2)

The result is approximately 8.89.

CGPA vs Percentage

CGPA and percentage are different ways of representing academic performance. The conversion between CGPA and percentage depends on the grading system and institution.

Therefore, students should check their school, college or university's official conversion rule before converting CGPA to percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does CGPA mean?

CGPA means Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents a student's overall academic performance using grade points.

How do I calculate CGPA with credits?

Multiply each subject's grade point by its credit value, add the results and divide by the total number of credits.

Can I calculate CGPA without credits?

Yes, if all subjects have equal weight, an ordinary average of the grade points can be used. However, if subjects have different credits, a credit-weighted calculation is more appropriate.

Is CGPA the same as percentage?

No. CGPA and percentage are different grading measures. The correct conversion depends on the institution's rules.
