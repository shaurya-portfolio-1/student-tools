How to Calculate Percentage from Marks

To calculate your percentage, divide your obtained marks by the total marks and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example

Suppose you obtained 450 marks out of 600 marks.

Percentage = (450 ÷ 600) × 100

Percentage = 75%
