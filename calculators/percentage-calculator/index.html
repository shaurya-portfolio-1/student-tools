How to Calculate Percentage from Marks

Percentage is a way of expressing a number out of 100. Students commonly use percentages to calculate exam results, school marks, college marks and test scores.

To calculate percentage from marks, divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: 450 out of 600

If you scored 450 marks out of 600:

(450 ÷ 600) × 100 = 75%

Therefore, 450 out of 600 is equal to 75%.

How to Calculate Marks from Percentage

If you know the target percentage and total marks, you can calculate the marks required to achieve that percentage.

Required Marks = (Percentage × Total Marks) ÷ 100

Example: 75% of 600

75% of 600 marks is:

(75 × 600) ÷ 100 = 450 marks

How to Calculate Percentage Increase

Percentage increase shows how much a value has increased compared with its original value.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: 100 to 150

If a value increases from 100 to 150, the increase is 50%.

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease

Percentage decrease shows how much a value has decreased compared with its original value.

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value - New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: 200 to 150

If a value decreases from 200 to 150, the decrease is 25%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate percentage from marks?

Divide obtained marks by total marks and multiply by 100. For example, 450 out of 600 is 75%.

What is 450 out of 600 as a percentage?

450 out of 600 is 75%.

How do I calculate marks from a percentage?

Multiply the percentage by the total marks and divide by 100. For example, 75% of 600 is 450 marks.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide the difference by the original value and multiply by 100.
