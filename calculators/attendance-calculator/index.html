How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Attendance Calculator Example

Suppose a student attended 60 classes out of 80 total classes.

(60 ÷ 80) × 100 = 75%

Therefore, the student's attendance is 75%.

Why Is Attendance Important?

Schools, colleges and universities may have minimum attendance requirements. Tracking your attendance helps you understand whether you are meeting your required percentage.
