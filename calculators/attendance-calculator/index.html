How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Example of Attendance Calculation

Suppose you attended 66 classes out of 100 total classes.

66 ÷ 100 × 100 = 66%

How Many Classes Do I Need to Attend?

If your current attendance is below your target, this calculator estimates how many additional consecutive classes you need to attend to reach your target.

How Many Classes Can I Miss?

If your current attendance is above your target, the calculator estimates how many additional future classes you can miss while keeping your attendance at or above your target.

These are additional future classes, not classes you have already attended or missed.

Important: Classes Are Not Days

The calculator counts individual classes, not calendar days. If you have five classes in one day, missing one day could mean missing five classes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does "classes I can miss" mean future classes?

Yes. It means additional future classes you can miss while remaining at or above your target attendance.

Does the calculator count days?

No. It counts individual classes or lectures.

What if my attendance is below my target?

The calculator tells you approximately how many consecutive classes you need to attend to reach your target.
