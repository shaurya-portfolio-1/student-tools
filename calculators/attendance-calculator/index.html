Your Current Attendance

How Attendance Is Calculated

Attendance percentage is calculated using:

Attendance % = (Attended Classes ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Classes You Can Miss

If your current attendance is already above your target, this calculator shows how many additional future classes you can miss while still staying at or above your target.

Classes You Need to Attend

If your current attendance is below your target, the calculator shows how many consecutive classes you need to attend to reach your target.

Important

The calculator counts individual classes, not days. If you have 5 classes in one day and miss the whole day, that means you missed 5 classes.
